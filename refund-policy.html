Digital Product Policy

Bzillion.Club memberships are digital products. Once your account is activated and you have access to the platform, the product has been delivered. Because digital products cannot be "returned," all sales are generally considered final.

However, we are committed to customer satisfaction. If you experience a genuine issue with our platform, we will work with you to resolve it fairly.

30-Day Consideration Window

We offer a 30-day window from your date of purchase to raise any concerns or issues. Refund requests must be submitted within this period to be considered.
